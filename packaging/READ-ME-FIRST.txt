MacClip - Clipboard history for macOS
=====================================

INSTALL
  1. Drag MacClip into the Applications folder (or just double-click MacClip
     and choose "Move to Applications").
  2. Open MacClip. Its icon appears in the menu bar (top right).
  3. When asked, allow Accessibility in System Settings > Privacy & Security
     > Accessibility. This lets MacClip paste for you. Without it, MacClip
     still copies the item and you press Command-V yourself.

USE
  Option + V (or click the menu-bar icon) opens your last 20 copies.
  Arrow keys + Return, a number 1-9, or a click pastes. Type to search.

IF macOS SAYS THE APP "CANNOT BE OPENED" OR "COULD NOT BE VERIFIED"
  MacClip is not signed by a paid Apple developer account, so macOS shows this
  warning on first launch. It is safe to open if you trust whoever gave it to you:
    a) Try to open MacClip once, then go to System Settings > Privacy & Security,
       scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to MacClip, then confirm.
  or
    b) In Terminal run:
         xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/MacClip.app
       and open MacClip again.

PRIVACY
  Everything stays on your Mac. MacClip has no network code. History is stored
  in ~/Library/Application Support/MacClip. Passwords copied from password
  managers that mark them as secret are never recorded.

UNINSTALL
  Quit MacClip from its menu-bar icon, delete it from Applications, and remove
  ~/Library/Application Support/MacClip.
